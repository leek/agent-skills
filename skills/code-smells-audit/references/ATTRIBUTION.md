This skill's reference material is a **derivative work** based on the
[Code Smells Catalog](https://codesmells.org/) by Marcel Jerzyk
([Luzkan/smells](https://github.com/Luzkan/smells), MIT licensed). Card
definitions are rewritten, detection heuristics for PHP/Laravel and TS/React
are original additions, and code examples are rewritten in PHP or TypeScript
(upstream examples are Python). The one-line smell descriptions in the index
are quoted verbatim from upstream's `meta.description` strings, and the
taxonomy (expanse, obstruction, occurrence), smell relations, aliases, and
refactoring vocabulary derive from upstream's research data.
